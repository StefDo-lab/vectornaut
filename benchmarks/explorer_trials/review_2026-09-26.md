# Independent review of explorer candidates (2026-09-26)

An independent reviewer (a fresh Claude instance acting as a marine-coatings expert, without access to the explorer runs) assessed eleven candidates from trials v1–v3 (`review_dossier.md`) with web searches for prior art and its own order-of-magnitude calculations (`review_calc.py`). Prior-art checks used web search only (no formal patent search; some full texts were blocked).

## Reference conditions

Ship at 15 kn: Cf ≈ 0.0015–0.002, u_τ ≈ 0.21–0.24 m/s, viscous length ≈ 4–5 µm, wall shear ≈ 45–60 Pa; ΔU+ = 1 ≈ 5–6 % friction change. Any "few %" texture needs features of a few to tens of µm, which slime fills within weeks; slime costs ~5–10 % (up to 20 %) per year.

## Shortlist

1. **H + G: groomable very soft foul-release coating** — tiles sized by the crust rule, supplied as an adhesive foil, combined with robotic grooming. First experiment (~3 months): bottlebrush/low-sol PDMS bed (~0.1 MPa, 350 µm) with 40 µm filled-silicone tiles, gaps filled flush; pseudobarnacle push-off, 50–100 grooming-brush cycles with sand slurry, cut test; then 3–6 months static immersion.
2. **K reframed: induction thermal pulse through the steel hull** from a grooming crawler, LCST chemistry optional; four-arm flow-cell test (heat, LCST+heat, brush, brush+heat) — kill if brush alone matches.
3. **J only as an add-on** to #1.

## Overall

Mostly recombinations of known ideas. Genuinely new elements found: the armouring rule for very soft FR beds (H/G) and using the steel hull as the induction target (K). Realistic value 0.5–1.5 % time-averaged friction plus coating life and grooming compatibility — incremental, patent-worthy-looking improvements, not step changes.

## What this says about the explorer

- The highest-scored v1 concepts (A, C: 77–78 points) were rejected by the reviewer; v1 scoring rewarded clean-surface model artefacts.
- After the v2/v3 changes (objective framing over service life, critic, requirement coverage), v3's top-ranked cluster (compliance-based release: H, the glacier/pack-ice skins) matches the reviewer's best concept, and the v2 grooming crust (G) forms the other half of the reviewer's recommended combination.
- The guided search produced both elements of the recommended combination; the unguided baselines did not.
