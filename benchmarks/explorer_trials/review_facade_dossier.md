# Candidate dossier: passive, bio-inspired cooling coatings / facade structures

Request: "Develop a bio-inspired, passive coating or facade structure for buildings in hot climates that clearly reduces the indoor heat load on summer days, needs no electricity or water supply, and stays weather-resistant for at least 20 years."

Objective as framed: summer-day heat flux into the conditioned interior through sun-exposed roofs and facades, averaged over 20 years of service including UV ageing, weathering and soiling. Conventional baseline: white acrylic/elastomeric cool coating in aged, soiled condition (solar reflectance SR 0.85 new, ~0.65–0.70 after 3 years; thermal emittance 0.90), repainted every ~10 years, on the same construction.

Candidates were generated by an AI idea-search system and screened with simple 1D heat-balance models. Authors' claims are given; check them.
